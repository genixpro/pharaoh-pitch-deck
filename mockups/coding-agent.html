Rationale

Over the last 14 days, 47 workstation endpoints have reported repeated DNS resolution timeouts for internal corp services (avg 8.3s, p99 22s). Investigation by the Endpoint Sentinel found the local systemd-resolved cache TTL was set to 30s — far below the upstream resolver's 600s minimum, causing constant re-resolution cascades.

Raising TTL to 300s aligns with corp DNS policy (KB-2104) and resolves the recurring pattern. Negative-cache TTL added to prevent thrash on failed lookups during VPN handoff. No impact on dynamic DNS records, which use direct queries.
